How people use our apps in real life

You don’t need a tech background. Madtales AI Audiobooks is for listening; Meetivo is for meetings. Here’s who tends to love each one.

For creators

Share your words in audio, too

Turn essays, chapters, or newsletters into something fans can hear on a walk or at the gym—without re-recording in a studio.

C1

Reach ears, not only eyes

Meet listeners where they already are—headphones on, phone in pocket.

C2

Anytime listening

Your audience can catch up when sitting down to read isn’t an option.

For teams

Stop losing track of meetings

Meetivo helps groups that talk a lot and still need everyone aligned afterward—without one person typing the whole time.

T1

One shared memory

Decisions and context stay visible for the whole group.

T2

Clear next steps

See tasks and owners without digging through chat history.

For you

Learn more, stress less

Use audiobooks for courses or books you never finish. If your job is meeting-heavy, Meetivo can shrink the mental load of remembering every detail.

I1

Easier studying

Let your eyes rest while your ears do the reading.

I2

Clearer planning

Short notes after calls help you decide what to do next.

Audiobooks shine when…

You’d rather listen than scroll

  • School or training material needs to fit into a busy day.
  • You save articles but never get around to reading them.
  • You want stories to feel closer, like someone reading to you.
  • You learn better by hearing than by staring at a page.
Meetivo shines when…

Calls pile up and details slip

  • Managers need quick recaps without writing them by hand.
  • Remote teammates miss context and ask the same questions twice.
  • Sales or support talks need a record everyone trusts.
  • Projects hand off between people and nothing should get lost.
