Separate sections per product

The audiobook app deals with text and audio you bring in. Meetivo deals with meetings, notes on your device, and summaries for teams. The details matter, so each product has its own section here—and we’ll add sections as we ship new apps.

Madtales AI Audiobooks

Privacy Policy

Madtales AI Audiobooks is built for immersive listening, so the policy centers on uploaded source material, generated audiobook files, and account support.

Reading and listening content Cloud sync aware

1. Scope

This section applies to users of Madtales AI Audiobooks and to related support or website interactions connected to the product.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, and sign-in information.
  • Uploaded documents, stories, or source files used to generate audiobooks.
  • Generated audiobook files and playback preferences needed to support the listening experience.
  • Usage analytics and diagnostics used to improve stability, voices, and language support.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate AI audiobooks and deliver core product functionality.
  • To maintain accounts, provide support, and respond to user requests.
  • To improve product quality, multilingual coverage, and performance.

4. Storage and Cloud Sync

Generated audiobook files may be stored in secure cloud infrastructure when sync or backup features are enabled so users can access their content across devices.

Where cloud sync is not enabled, content handling is limited to the storage needed to operate the service experience.

5. Third-Party Processing

We may use infrastructure, storage, text-to-speech, analytics, and AI service providers to operate the product. Their services remain subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing and User Rights

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We share data only with service providers needed to run the product or when required by law.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting contact@madtalesai.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the product indicates acceptance of the latest version.

Meetivo

Privacy Policy

Meetivo is designed around meeting intelligence, so the policy emphasizes local meeting data storage, third-party AI processing, and global privacy rights.

Last updated 26 March 2026 Local-first meeting data

1. Scope

This policy applies to Meetivo users globally and is intended to align with applicable privacy frameworks, including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and the India DPDP Act.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, and Google or Apple sign-in data.
  • Limited technical data like device type, app events, and crash logs, anonymized or aggregated where possible.
  • Meeting data including recordings, transcripts, and summaries generated during use of the product.

3. Where Meeting Data Lives

Meetivo does not store meeting recordings, transcripts, or summaries on Madtales-owned central servers.

This meeting data is stored locally on the user's device.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide AI-powered meeting summaries and structured outputs.
  • To operate and maintain the app.
  • To improve features, performance, and support quality.

5. Third-Party Processing

To deliver core functionality, meeting data may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers for processing.

  • These providers act as independent processors.
  • Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.
  • Madtales does not control or assume liability for third-party data practices beyond reasonable due diligence.

6. Retention, Sharing, and Rights

  • Account-related data is retained only as long as needed to provide services.
  • We do not sell personal data and share data only for processing needs or legal obligations.
  • Depending on jurisdiction, users may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, and grievance redressal.

7. Security and International Transfers

We use industry-standard safeguards, but no system can be completely secure. If you access Meetivo outside your country of residence, data may be processed in other jurisdictions used by service providers.

8. Children and Contact

Meetivo is not intended for children under 13, or the equivalent age in the relevant jurisdiction. For privacy requests, contact contact@madtalesai.com.

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of Meetivo constitutes acceptance of the updated version.
